# Release notes

## Standalone v1.0.0 — 18 September 2026

- Initial offline package derived from the published Prophylactic Pelvic Nodal Irradiation NNT Calculator.
- Includes POP-RT efficacy, late toxicity and QOL context.
- Includes SPPORT efficacy, acute and late toxicity, and the 2026 patient-reported QOL analysis.
- Shows unrounded NNT/NNH to two decimal places and conventional whole-patient rounding.
- Shows the underlying absolute-difference subtraction below each trial result.
- Adds custom event-free outcome and adverse-event calculations.
- Adds browser-only privacy statement, mobile/PWA metadata, subfolder-safe service worker and offline cache.
- Adds automated known-answer validation, user guide, methodology/references and clinical disclaimer.

### Evidence snapshot

Evidence and links were checked in September 2026. Trial estimates are intentionally fixed to preserve reproducibility; review current guidance and later publications before clinical use.
